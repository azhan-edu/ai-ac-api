# One-Day Meal Plan for Vegetarian Weightlifter

Here is a one-day meal plan for a 42-year-old female athlete aiming to develop strength and power for weightlifting with optimal muscle recovery:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 2,200 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (130g), Fat (65g), Carbs (280g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Oatmeal (80g dry weight) with ground flaxseed (15g), banana (120g), and whole milk (240g).
    * Protein: 14g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 65g
    * *Cost: ~$1.20*

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:00 AM):** Greek yogurt (200g, plain) with granola (40g) and berries (80g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 5g, Carbs: 35g
    * *Cost: ~$1.50*

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Lentil and vegetable curry with spinach (100g), lentils (150g cooked), onion (60g), and tomato (80g) served with brown rice (150g cooked).
    * Protein: 22g, Fat: 6g, Carbs: 75g
    * *Cost: ~$2.00*

* **Pre-Workout Snack (3:30 PM):** Whole wheat bread (60g) with peanut butter (25g) and honey (15g).
    * Protein: 9g, Fat: 13g, Carbs: 35g
    * *Cost: ~$0.80*

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Baked tofu (180g) with roasted sweet potato (200g) and steamed broccoli (150g). Olive oil for cooking (10g).
    * Protein: 35g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 50g
    * *Cost: ~$2.50*

* **Evening Snack (9:00 PM):** Cottage cheese (150g, low-fat) with sunflower seeds (20g).
    * Protein: 27g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 5g
    * *Cost: ~$1.00*
